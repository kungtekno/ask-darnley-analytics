Ask Darnley Analytics

Channel performance reports for the channel's owner

Ask Darnley Analytics is a private reporting tool. The owner of a YouTube channel connects that channel, and Ask Darnley Analytics reads the channel's own statistics and analytics to produce performance reports: how each video is doing, how long viewers keep watching, and where the views come from.

What it asks for, and why

Ask Darnley Analytics asks for read-only access to the connected channel: its list of videos, their public statistics, and its YouTube Analytics reports. This is the data the reports are built from. Ask Darnley Analytics cannot upload, edit or delete videos, and it does not read email, contacts or any other Google service.

Who can use it

Ask Darnley Analytics is used only by the channel owner who authorised it. There is no sign-up and no public service.

Privacy Policy

Ask Darnley Analytics · Effective 2026-11-16

Ask Darnley Analytics is a private reporting tool used by the owner of a YouTube channel to read that channel's own statistics and analytics. This policy explains what Ask Darnley Analytics accesses, how it is used and stored, and how to remove it.

YouTube API Services

Ask Darnley Analytics uses YouTube API Services. By using Ask Darnley Analytics you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data that Google processes is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Information Ask Darnley Analytics accesses

Only after you authorise it with your Google Account, and only for the channel you connect, Ask Darnley Analytics reads:

  • channel and video details: channel name and ID, video IDs, titles and publish dates;
  • video statistics: views, likes and comment counts;
  • YouTube Analytics reports for the channel: views, engaged views, watch time, average view duration, audience retention, traffic sources, and subscribers gained and lost.

Access is read-only. Ask Darnley Analytics does not receive your password, and it does not access email, contacts, files or any other Google service.

How the information is used

The information is used for one purpose: producing performance reports for the channel owner. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not combined with data about other people.

Sharing

The information is not shared with other parties, with one exception: the channel owner may have the stored reports analysed by automated analysis software, including third-party AI services, acting on the owner's instructions. Ask Darnley Analytics's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

The information is stored on the channel owner's own computer. It is kept while the authorisation is active. Ask Darnley Analytics checks at least every 30 days that the authorisation is still valid and that each video still exists; data for deleted videos is removed. If access is revoked, all stored data for that channel is deleted within 30 calendar days.

Cookies and device data

Ask Darnley Analytics does not place cookies or similar technology on your devices, and it does not show advertising or other third-party content. This website is a set of static pages; its hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses.

Removing access

You can revoke Ask Darnley Analytics's access at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. You can also ask for stored data to be deleted by writing to the address below.
